Google Drive "Incoming Circulars" & Auto-Sync Guide

You have created your Google Drive folder and published your Google Sheet! Here is how the complete pipeline works end-to-end:

1. Google Drive Folder Watcher ("Incoming Circulars")

Open your tracking Google Sheet → click Extensions → Apps Script → paste the copied Drive Watcher Script. Set a Time-Driven Trigger (every 5 or 15 minutes) on watchIncomingCircularsFolder.

  • Automatically detects new PDFs, Word docs, or text notices dropped into your "Incoming Circulars" (or "Incoming Circulsrs") folder.
  • Extracts the circular title, date, audience, and venue, and appends a row directly into your Google Sheet.
2. Live CSV Sync & Date-Range Retrieval Connected

Paste your Google Sheet's published .csv link in the Google Sheets Sync & Groq AI tab.

  • Every synced schedule is also saved in your local historical vault so you can recall past schedules anytime using the From Date and To Date buttons.

Circular Drop-Box Automation Pipelines

To turn this scheduler into a live automated drop-box where circulars are sent automatically from school/college administration, you can connect it using any of these 3 plug-and-play workflows:

1. Dedicated Ingestion Email Address Recommended

Set up a dedicated email forwarding address like circulars@yourschool.edu.

  • Any incoming PDF or circular email body is sent to a webhook (Zapier, Make, or Python IMAP script).
  • The script runs Gemini / LLM parsing (same prompt logic as our web app) to extract {Title, Date, Deadline, Audience, Venue}.
  • Events automatically sync to your Google Calendar & this dashboard!
2. Cloud Folder "Drop-Box" (Drive / Dropbox) Google Drive

Create a Google Drive folder named "Incoming Circulars" on your phone/desktop.

  • Whenever someone WhatsApps or shares a circular PDF, you simply save/move it to this folder.
  • A Google Apps Script triggers on new file upload, runs OCR / text extraction, and pushes the event to your calendar.
3. WhatsApp or Telegram "Drop" Bot Instant Mobile

Forward forwarded circulars directly to a private Telegram or WhatsApp bot.

  • The bot accepts forwarded photos/PDFs of notices.
  • Replies with: "Extracted: Annual Sports Day on Nov 14 at 9 AM for All Students. Added to your schedule!"
